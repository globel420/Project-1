How the LEDs work

Each light has a value.

Add the values of the lights that are on. That is the number.

showBinary() uses bitRead() to read each position. A 1 turns its light on; a 0 turns it off.

13 uses the 8, 4, and 1 lights: 01101 in binary.
The math behind reading a bit

For a positive LED value, divide the count by that value and drop the decimal part. An odd result means 1; an even result means 0. This is the arithmetic equivalent of selecting that bit.

incrementCounter() adds one after a short press and release. After 31, it returns to 0.

Each light answers a question.

Pressed means true. Released means false. A true result turns its light on.

Click A or B to show that button pressed or released.

Neither button is pressed, so only NOT A is on.

runProjectB() reads A and B, checks these four conditions, and sends each result to its LED with digitalWrite().
